The Studio

Our Philosophy

Aura merges minimalist aesthetics with warm, tactile materials to create environments that are both striking and profoundly serene. We design spaces that elevate the human experience.

Intentional Living Through Design

We believe that your physical space fundamentally shapes your inner landscape. Our approach focuses on stripping away the non-essential to reveal the inherent beauty of natural light, authentic materials, and considered proportions.

Every Aura project begins with a deep dive into the lives of the people who will inhabit the space. We study routines, quiet moments, and aspirations, translating these intangible qualities into architectural language.

By blending modern restraint with organic warmth, we craft sanctuaries that offer respite from the noise of the outside world—spaces that truly breathe.

Our Process

True luxury lies in the details. We collaborate intimately with master artisans, sourcing the finest sustainable materials to ensure every finish and fixture feels meticulously curated.

From the initial conceptual sketches to the final installation of bespoke furniture, our process is a dialogue between form and function, resulting in spaces that are unequivocally yours.

"Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication."

Our multidisciplinary team of architects, interior designers, and artisans work symbiotically to ensure every detail, from the macro layout to the micro texture of a custom cabinet pull, is executed with uncompromising precision.

Founded in 2018 in Copenhagen, Aura Design Studio was born out of a desire to bridge the gap between stark modernism and lived-in warmth. We draw inspiration from the raw beauty of Scandinavian landscapes, translating organic forms and natural textures into refined urban sanctuaries.

Sustainability & Craft

We are fiercely committed to ecological responsibility. Our studio prioritizes working with local craftsmen, utilizing reclaimed woods, natural stone off-cuts, and non-toxic finishes. We believe that true luxury should not come at the expense of our environment, and our designs are built to last generations, circumventing the cycle of fast furniture.
