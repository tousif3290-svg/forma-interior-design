THE STUDIO

Forma is an internationally acclaimed interior design studio specializing in minimalist, timeless residential spaces. We believe that the environment you inhabit profoundly shapes your daily experience.

Our Story

Founded in 2018 in the heart of New York City, Forma was born out of a desire to strip away the unnecessary and focus on the essential. Our founders recognized that modern life is increasingly cluttered and chaotic, and that our homes should be the ultimate antidote.

What started as a boutique consultancy has grown into a full-service design firm, taking on high-end residential projects across North America and Europe. Despite our growth, our core ethos remains unchanged: less is always more, provided the execution is flawless.

Our Approach

We approach every space as a blank canvas, deeply considering its architectural context, natural light, and the unique lifestyle of the people who will live there. We do not impose a strict "house style," but rather apply our minimalist principles to our clients' unique tastes.

Sustainability is also at the forefront of our minds. We source materials responsibly, partner with local artisans, and favor vintage or custom-built pieces over mass-produced furniture. The result is a home that not only looks beautiful but feels fundamentally good to live in.
